# Venue research (agent report, 2026-08-06)

## Ranked recommendation

1. **CHANCE — "Taking a Chance in the Classroom" column.** Column articles are
   explicitly exempt from peer review; opinion is house style; the column's editors
   (Mine Cetinkaya-Rundel & Maria Tackett) and readers are exactly people who teach
   statistics. Genre precedent: Bray, Cetinkaya-Rundel & Stangl, "Five Concrete
   Reasons Your Students Should Be Learning to Analyze Data in the Reproducible
   Paradigm," CHANCE 27(3), 2014. Length 10-15 double-spaced pages (~2,500-4,000
   words), no abstract, no citations -- "Further Reading" list of ~5.
2. **Significance — "In Practice" section.** 1,000-2,500 words, editorial-board
   vetting not full refereeing, <=5 end references, Word-only submission to
   significance@rss.org.uk. Genre precedent: Matthews, "The p-value statement,
   five years on," Significance 18(3), 2021.
3. **JSDSE.** 100% educator audience, citable diamond-OA article, but no protected
   opinion lane: full double-blind review as a position/reflection paper. Slowest,
   riskiest. Precedent: Cetinkaya-Rundel & Ellison, "A Fresh Look at Introductory
   Data Science," JSDSE 29:sup1, 2021.

Fast fallback: **Amstat News "Statistician's View"** (~1,200-1,800 words, staff
editorial review, ~1 month to print, submit megan@amstat.org) -- as a short
companion provocation pointing at the longer piece.

Avoid: SERJ (no category), Mathematical Intelligencer (wrong audience), HDSR
(proposal-first, education column is K-12), Teaching Statistics (audience skews
school-level), TAS Commentaries (conflicted while Marginally Useful is under
review there).

## Full table (abridged)

- JSDSE: no word limit, double-blind, diamond OA, months.
- Teaching Statistics (Wiley): refereed double-blind, no set length, school-tertiary
  audience.
- CHANCE: column exempt from peer review; quarterly; subscription magazine, no fees.
- Significance: sections In Brief 500-1,000 / In Detail 2,000-3,000 / In Practice
  1,000-2,500 / website 500-1,500; no LaTeX.
- SERJ: research formats only, opinion by prior inquiry; diamond OA; slow.
- HDSR: proposal-first, columns 1,500-2,500, two-stage review.
- Mathematical Intelligencer "Viewpoint": no length limit, editor discretion,
  controversy welcome; math audience; OA APC $3,290 (optional).
- Amstat News: ~600 words/page; staff edit only; fastest; not archival.

Key links: chance.amstat.org/contact/submit-an-article/;
significancemagazine.com Notes for Contributors; magazine.amstat.org/submission-instructions/.
